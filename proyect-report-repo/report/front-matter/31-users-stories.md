# User Stories Completas

| Epic ID | Epic Title | Epic Description |
|------|-------------|------------------|
| E-01 | IoT Security and Risk Monitoring | **As a** gas company administrator, <br>**I want** the platform to monitor gas leaks, alerts, incidents, and warehouse sensor status in real time, <br>**so that** operational risks can be detected and addressed quickly to protect personnel, facilities, and inventory. |
| E-02 | Inventory and Warehouse Management | **As a** logistics and inventory manager, <br>**I want** to digitally manage the registration, tracking, validation, and history of gas cylinder inventory movements, <br>**so that** stock information remains accurate, updated, and fully traceable across warehouses and distributors. |
| E-03 | Distribution and Delivery Control | **As a** logistics supervisor, <br>**I want** to manage and monitor deliveries, assigned personnel, vehicles, routes, and delivery statuses in real time, <br>**so that** distribution operations are organized, traceable, and efficient. |
| E-04 | Operational Analytics and Reporting | **As an** administrative supervisor, <br>**I want** to visualize operational summaries, trends, comparisons, dashboards, and customizable reports, <br>**so that** I can analyze business performance, monitor workloads, and make informed operational decisions. |
| E-05 | User, Access, and Business Management | **As a** system administrator, <br>**I want** to manage users, permissions, audit records, scalability options, and distributor financial operations such as sales and credit tracking, <br>**so that** the platform remains secure, scalable, centralized, and reliable for all users. |
